Your Feedback Management System,Your response our inspiration

  • PRISM is a very simple feedback management system built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It is used mainly by students and one admin: students log in to share their opinions about the college, and the admin logs in to read them.
  • The admin's role is to check the feedback, and only the admin can view it. There are no graphs or charts, just a plain textual view of the reviews. The student's role is to give a review by choosing one of the fixed feedback categories, such as faculty and teaching, library services or infrastructure, and submitting it. Students can only give feedback, not see what others have written.
